07 · Personal & Academic Project

Hospital Readmission Risk

Turning fragmented EHR history into an actionable early-warning signal

  • PostgreSQL
  • Python
  • Scikit-learn
  • Feature Engineering
  • Streamlit
Open live app ↗GitHub ↗

Business value

The model reached 0.82 ROC-AUC and produced a ranked high-risk list for care-coordination prioritization.

01 · Business problem

Patient data was spread across 16 relational tables with duplicate IDs, overlapping dates, and inconsistent care episodes.

02 · My approach

I resolved identity and time alignment first, engineered clinically meaningful features, and used leakage-safe evaluation.

03 · What I built

PostgreSQL integration, keyed joins, date alignment, clinically informed imputation, 22 features, stratified validation, and Streamlit.

What changed my thinking

The corrections, tradeoffsand decisions behind it.

  1. 01

    Integration was harder than modeling

    The same patient appeared across multiple tables and care episodes with conflicting timing.

  2. 02

    Leakage changed the evaluation

    Future information had to be removed before performance could be trusted.

  3. 03

    A score was not enough

    The final output emphasized ranked patients and understandable risk factors.
