Traxidy

Business value

The analysis surfaced resource constraints and scope changes as major delay drivers, helping leadership prioritize interventions.

01 · Business problem

Project performance data lived in 76 SQL tables, and teams defined delay and status differently. Leadership lacked a consistent cross-project view.

02 · My approach

I reduced the data model, standardized GYR and delay definitions, and focused the analysis on actionable root causes.

03 · What I built

Eleven analytical tables, operational KPIs, Random Forest and LSTM models, Tableau dashboards, and a chatbot prototype.

What changed my thinking

The corrections, tradeoffsand decisions behind it.

  1. 01

    More tables did not create more insight

    The key move was reducing 76 scattered tables into 11 usable structures.

  2. 02

    Definitions had to be fixed first

    A model cannot learn consistently when teams label risk differently.

  3. 03

    The chatbot was an access layer

    Its value came from making trusted metrics easier to reach, not from being the main product.
